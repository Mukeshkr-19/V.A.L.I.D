# Documentation presentation

Lead with outcomes, then runnable setup, concrete review output, and authority boundaries. Use the banner and compact local technology badges in docs/assets. Badges describe technology or supported scope; they must not imply an unverified deployment, sponsor approval, or test result.

Use short paragraphs, flat navigation, and tables for contracts. Distinguish generated advice, detector evidence, and the deterministic gate. Display basic review explicitly when no useful AI advice is accepted. Keep evidence below the fix plan and label proposed edits as examples requiring human review.

SVG assets use static shapes and system fonts, with no scripts, embedded HTML, external resources, or tracking pixels.
